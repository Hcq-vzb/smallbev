Bottle Drying Tunnel

Published 2026-11-16 · KIWL Machine

Specifying a bottle drying tunnel for small beverage startups is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

A bottle drying tunnel earns a place on the small beverage startups production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 2691–3691 units per hour on 20 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask what a supplier is not willing to write down. The answer, on a bottle drying tunnel, is usually the tolerance at the low end of the range: this build holds 3.0 ml at 2691 units per hour, not only at 3691.

Integration With Your Line

A bottle drying tunnel that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1200 mm working width, which is what lets the small beverage startups production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A bottle drying tunnel at 2691–3691 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 20 L product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the small beverage startups operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

For a small beverage startups operation the maintenance question for a bottle drying tunnel is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the bottle drying tunnel?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 2691 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the bottle drying tunnel handle a 20 L product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 8-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 7-inch colour touch.

Is a 316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 20 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
