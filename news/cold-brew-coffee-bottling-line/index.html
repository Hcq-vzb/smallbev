Cold Brew Coffee Bottling Line

Published 2026-11-27 · KIWL Machine

A cold brew coffee bottling line for small beverage startups is judged on the specification and then lived with for years. This guide covers what to ask for, what to verify on the floor, and what to keep in stock afterwards.

What This Equipment Does

Where a cold brew coffee bottling line is judged is at the far end of the line, in the packed product. The reference build puts 20 L containers onto the line at 5311–6811 units per hour with ±3.0 ml repeatability, and that is the number to verify.

Key Technical Specifications

Do not compare a cold brew coffee bottling line on rated capacity alone. A 5311–6811 bottles per hour machine with ±3.0 ml tolerance and 304/316L stainless steel contact parts will out-produce a faster machine with looser accuracy over a full shift.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the small beverage startups team will adopt the routine — and a cold brew coffee bottling line with a 12-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the cold brew coffee bottling line against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 12 minutes per format change and 5311 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a cold brew coffee bottling line are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a small beverage startups shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the cold brew coffee bottling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 2400 × 3600 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

Can the cold brew coffee bottling line run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 20 L product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

What accuracy can this cold brew coffee bottling line hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±3.0 ml on 20 L containers at 5311–6811 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
