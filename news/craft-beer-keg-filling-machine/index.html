Craft Beer Keg Filling Machine

Published 2026-10-24 · KIWL Machine

Three questions decide most craft beer keg filling machine purchases, and they are the same for every small beverage startups operation: what rate, what accuracy, and what happens when it stops. This guide takes them in that order.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of a craft beer keg filling machine is not its peak rate but its behaviour at 19096 bottles per hour on a 330 ml format, where product density shifts and viscosity climbs.

Key Technical Specifications

A quotation for a craft beer keg filling machine is easiest to compare when it is reduced to four numbers: 0.5 ml tolerance, 19096–23596 units per hour, 12–17 kW and 1200 × 3600 × 3200 mm. Everything else follows from the reference build's 316L stainless steel contact parts and 8-minute changeover.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This craft beer keg filling machine already handles a standard encoder interface and a 1200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Include the service cost and the format kit cost before concluding. A craft beer keg filling machine with a 7-inch colour touch and a 8-minute changeover has a predictable running cost, which is worth more to a small beverage startups buyer than a lower purchase price.

Maintenance and Service

The wear items on this craft beer keg filling machine are the seals, the drive and the guide rails. None of them should fail without warning, and none of them should require a week of lead time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the craft beer keg filling machine run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 330 ml product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

Can the craft beer keg filling machine be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 19096–23596 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 330 ml range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
