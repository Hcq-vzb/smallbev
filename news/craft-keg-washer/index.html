Craft Keg Washer

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

A craft keg washer for small beverage startups is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

Ask a small beverage startups plant engineer to describe their best-performing craft keg washer and they will describe the one that runs for weeks without intervention. This configuration is specified at 16430–20930 units per hour on 500 ml can product with that as the design target.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This craft keg washer draws 15–20 kW across a 3200 × 2200 × 3200 mm footprint with 304/316L stainless steel contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

A craft keg washer that shares its control protocol with the rest of the small beverage startups production line can be called up by a recipe change. That is what the standard encoder interface on this build is for.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the craft keg washer against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 20 minutes per format change and 16430 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Remote support matters for a craft keg washer because the fastest diagnoses come from the actual machine data: a 4-inch HMI that records the last 20 minutes of a fault tells you more than a phone call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 4-inch HMI stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for small beverage startups operations running audits.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

What does the craft keg washer do about product waste at start-up?

The metering system holds the setpoint from the first measurable dose rather than ramping from empty, which is where most give-away occurs.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
