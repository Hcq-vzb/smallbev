Frequently Asked Questions

Does the edible oil bottle filling machine small need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1800 × 1900 × 2100 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

How long does a format changeover take on this edible oil bottle filling machine small?

With a standardised guide set the changeover is around 15 minutes. The first change on a new 330 ml format takes longer because the guide set has to be set and taught.

Can the edible oil bottle filling machine small be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 6082–7582 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 330 ml range before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?
