Kombucha Bottling Equipment

Published 2026-11-19 · KIWL Machine

The purpose of this guide is to shorten the kombucha bottling equipment decision for a small beverage startups buyer. Rate first, then the specification points, then the acceptance checks that turn a quotation into a working machine.

What This Equipment Does

Because a kombucha bottling equipment meters by volume or servo micro-metering, the same unit can be reconfigured for 8 oz format, and the changeover is limited to the guide set rather than the whole machine.

Key Technical Specifications

This kombucha bottling equipment draws 32–43 kW and occupies 3200 × 1900 × 3200 mm, uses food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and holds a 1.0 ml tolerance on 8 oz product.

Integration With Your Line

The costliest mistake is treating a kombucha bottling equipment as a standalone machine. Once it accepts a line encoder signal and works across a 3200 mm working width, it becomes part of the small beverage startups control loop and recipe changes stop being manual.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a kombucha bottling equipment is arithmetic: every unit of 1.0 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 10-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the small beverage startups production line.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the kombucha bottling equipment is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 3723–5223 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the kombucha bottling equipment need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1900 × 3200 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

What spare parts should be held for the kombucha bottling equipment?

Seals, drive belt and guide rails are the wear items. For a line running 3723 bottles per hour, holding these on site is what protects availability.

Can the kombucha bottling equipment run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 8 oz product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
