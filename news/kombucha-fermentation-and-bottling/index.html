Kombucha Fermentation And Bottling

Published 2026-11-26 · KIWL Machine

Everything that follows concerns a single machine: a kombucha fermentation and bottling for small beverage startups. The rate, the materials, the control system, the changeover and the acceptance checks — presented so that a technical buyer and an operations manager can use the same document.

What This Equipment Does

This configuration is built around 330 ml can containers, which removes the format-guessing that usually slows small beverage startups commissioning. Changeover then means swapping the guide set, not re-qualifying the whole line.

Key Technical Specifications

The footprint of 3200 × 1400 × 2400 mm and a 1.5–2.5 kW draw often decide whether a kombucha fermentation and bottling fits an existing small beverage startups hall, so request the GA drawing before comparing quotations.

Integration With Your Line

A kombucha fermentation and bottling that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width, which is what lets the small beverage startups production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the kombucha fermentation and bottling on total cost over the equipment life, not on the purchase price. A unit with 316L stainless steel contact parts and ±3.0 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same small beverage startups production line.

Maintenance and Service

Train the people who run the shift, not only the commissioning engineer. On this kombucha fermentation and bottling the wear items are simple to identify — seals, belt, guide rails — and most small beverage startups downtime is caused by a part that was available but not swapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the kombucha fermentation and bottling need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 1400 × 2400 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

What is the actual connected load of the kombucha fermentation and bottling?

The reference build draws 1.5–2.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

Can the kombucha fermentation and bottling run plastic and glass at the same time?

This configuration handles 330 ml can product on the specified format. Mixing materials usually means a cleaning and verification step between campaigns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
