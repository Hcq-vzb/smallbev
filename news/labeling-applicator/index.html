Labeling Applicator

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

The labeling applicator is easier to specify than most small beverage startups buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

A labeling applicator should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 330 ml can product at 17793–22293 units per hour, which is the span most small beverage startups production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Ask for the electrical single-line diagram and the duty cycle, not only the power total. This labeling applicator draws 41–56 kW across a 1200 × 3600 × 2800 mm footprint with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a labeling applicator can absorb. With 17793–22293 units per hour and a 10-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare a labeling applicator against the cost of the alternative rather than against zero. The manual step it removes at 17793 units per hour usually outweighs the purchase price inside the first operating year for a small beverage startups operation.

Maintenance and Service

For small beverage startups operations the practical measure of after-sales support is how quickly a labeling applicator returns to 17793 units per hour after a fault — which depends on part availability, not on the number of people in the service team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this labeling applicator?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 10 minutes. The first change to a new 330 ml can format takes longer.

What is the delivery lead time for the labeling applicator?

Lead time depends on the control package and the format tooling. Ask for the date with the order, not separately, so the 330 ml can set is included.

How does the labeling applicator behave if the upstream stops?

The buffer upstream of the labeling applicator determines the recovery. The machine itself restarts into the stored recipe once the upstream signal returns.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
