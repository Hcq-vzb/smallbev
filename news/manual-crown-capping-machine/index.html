What This Equipment Does

A manual crown capping machine should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 330 ml can product at 2454–3454 units per hour, which is the span most small beverage startups production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Throughput on a manual crown capping machine is only half the specification. Add the 15-minute format changeover, the 0.5–0.8 N·m window and the 316L stainless steel contact parts, and the comparison against another machine becomes meaningful.

Integration With Your Line

A servo head on this manual crown capping machine can serve as the master clock of a small block, which is often simpler than synchronising it to an upstream machine on the small beverage startups production line.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Reject reduction is the quiet benefit. Holding 0.5–0.8 N·m at 2454–3454 cans per minute removes an entire category of small beverage startups downtime that rarely appears in the quotation.

Maintenance and Service

Train the shift team to read the seam reject rate from the manual crown capping machine rather than waiting for a customer complaint. At 0.5–0.8 N·m the trend line is visible well before it becomes a quality incident.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the manual crown capping machine integrate with an existing line?

It accepts a line encoder signal and can be indexed directly against the upstream conveyor, so it runs under the line PLC rather than as an isolated unit.

What is the connected load of the manual crown capping machine?

The reference build draws 17–24 kW with a servo rotary head. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement before ordering.

How long does a format changeover take?

Around 15 minutes with standardised tooling. Budget more for the first change to a new 330 ml can format until the tooling set is dialled in.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
