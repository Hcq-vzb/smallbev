Mini Keg Filling Line

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

A mini keg filling line for small beverage startups is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

Before a mini keg filling line is quoted, the small beverage startups team should be able to answer three questions: what rate, what container, what tolerance. This configuration answers them as 5784–7284 units per hour, 60–120 mm diameter product and ±0.5 ml.

Key Technical Specifications

Rated capacity of 5784–7284 bottles per hour is only meaningful alongside the food-grade 304 stainless steel contact-part grade and the control system, because the cheaper versions of each trade directly against the dose tolerance.

Integration With Your Line

Integration work on a mini keg filling line is mostly deciding who owns what. Once the encoder, the cleaning route and the recipe store are agreed with the small beverage startups production line, commissioning stops being a negotiation.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 12-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the small beverage startups production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a mini keg filling line with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and 5784–7284 bottles per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the mini keg filling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1400 × 2800 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

Can the mini keg filling line be upgraded to higher speed later?

Within the 5784–7284 band the drive and control have headroom. Moving beyond it means a different metering head, so plan the 60–120 mm diameter range before ordering.

What accuracy can this mini keg filling line hold in real production?

The reference build holds ±0.5 ml on 60–120 mm diameter containers at 5784–7284 bottles per hour. In a real line the number to confirm is the deviation over a full shift rather than a short test.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
