Mini Water Treatment System

Published 2026-10-26 · KIWL Machine

The mini water treatment system is easier to specify than most small beverage startups buyers expect, because the hard questions have already been asked by other plants. This guide sets out those questions and the answers, in the form of figures you can hold a supplier to.

What This Equipment Does

What a mini water treatment system does to the small beverage startups production line is measurable at the packing end. At 1123–1473 units per hour for 20 L product, the shift output stops depending on how the previous hour went.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two mini water treatment system quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies 316L stainless steel surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 0.3 ml tolerance and a 12-minute changeover across 20 L format.

Integration With Your Line

A mini water treatment system that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1800 mm working width, which is what lets the small beverage startups production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity above contracted demand is exposure, not return. Size the mini water treatment system to real 20 L volume, then let the 0.3 ml tolerance and the 1123–1473 units per hour figure do the work on yield.

Maintenance and Service

Serviceability is a specification, not an afterthought. On this mini water treatment system the seals, sensors and drives are accessible without dismantling the frame, which is what makes a 12-minute routine service realistic on a 1123 unit per hour line.

Frequently Asked Questions

What spare parts should be held for the mini water treatment system?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 1123 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Is a 316L stainless steel contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 20 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

What is the actual connected load of the mini water treatment system?

The reference build draws 32–43 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
