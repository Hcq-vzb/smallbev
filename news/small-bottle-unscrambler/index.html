Frequently Asked Questions

Can the small bottle unscrambler be upgraded later?

Within the 15620–20120 band the drive and control have headroom. Going beyond it means a different configuration, so plan the 500 ml can range before ordering.

How are settings recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores a recipe per format and the parameters can be exported, which matters for small beverage startups operations running audits.

Does the small bottle unscrambler need a special foundation?

No special foundation beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 3200 × 2200 × 2400 mm GA drawing.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
