The figures in this guide are the ones a supplier should be held to in writing. For a small juice extraction machine in small beverage startups production, they cover rate, tolerance, materials, control, changeover and acceptance.

What This Equipment Does

Because a small juice extraction machine handles a defined step for small beverage startups, it can be isolated, measured and improved. This build is specified for 12 oz product at 1604–2104 units per hour, with changeover handled by configuration rather than by hand.

Key Technical Specifications

Where two small juice extraction machine quotations look identical, the difference is in the contact parts and the control platform. This reference build specifies 304/316L stainless steel surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 0.5 ml tolerance and a 12-minute changeover across 12 oz format.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the small juice extraction machine does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing small beverage startups conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Size the small juice extraction machine against peak demand with modest headroom, then model payback from labour, yield and downtime rather than from price. At 1604–2104 units per hour a typical small beverage startups operation recovers the investment within 12–36 months at regional scale.

Maintenance and Service

Preventive work on the small juice extraction machine is concentrated on the wear parts, and the recommended interval is derived from the 1604–2104 duty range rather than from a generic calendar figure.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a format changeover take on this small juice extraction machine?

With standardised tooling the changeover is around 12 minutes. The first change to a new 12 oz format takes longer.

Can the small juice extraction machine handle a 12 oz product change without full cleaning?

Changeover between compatible formats is a 12-minute operation. A material change needs the cleaning cycle, and the sequence is documented on the 10-inch colour touch.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
