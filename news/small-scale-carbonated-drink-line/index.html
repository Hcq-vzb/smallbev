Small Scale Carbonated Drink Line

Published 2026-10-27 · KIWL Machine

The difference between a workable small scale carbonated drink line and an expensive mistake usually shows up six months later, in downtime and in service calls. This guide covers the specification points that decide the outcome for small beverage startups, in the order they are best resolved.

What This Equipment Does

A small scale carbonated drink line should be judged on the range it handles, not the peak it advertises. This configuration covers 20 L product at 1742–2242 units per hour, which is the span most small beverage startups production lines actually demand.

Key Technical Specifications

Capacity without conditions is not a specification. 1742–2242 units per hour means little without 20 L product, a 3.0 ml tolerance and a 12-minute changeover — all three stated for this small scale carbonated drink line.

Integration With Your Line

The upstream buffer decides how much downtime a small scale carbonated drink line can absorb. With 1742–2242 units per hour and a 12-minute changeover, the buffer has to cover the change rather than the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

A 12-minute changeover should be priced into the ROI, because on the small beverage startups production line that switches formats often it represents output that is otherwise lost rather than merely inconvenient.

Maintenance and Service

For a small beverage startups operation the maintenance question for a small scale carbonated drink line is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the actual connected load of the small scale carbonated drink line?

The reference build draws 3–4 kW. Confirm starting current and supply arrangement at the site.

How is support handled after commissioning?

Remote support plus a documented spare parts list. Ask for the response time in writing before ordering.

Is a SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact part really necessary?

It determines the cleaning chemistry and the wear life. For corrosive or high-viscosity 20 L product the standard grade is not appropriate.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
