Small Scale Juice Bottling Line

Published 2026-10-22 · KIWL Machine

A small scale juice bottling line for small beverage startups is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

The reason to specify a small scale juice bottling line carefully is that the cost of a poor fit is paid over years. This configuration is built for 500 ml can containers, 5424–6924 units per hour and a 1.5 ml tolerance, so the fit is decided before the order, not after commissioning.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated capacity, contact-part material, control architecture and changeover time rather than headline speed. This reference small scale juice bottling line uses SUS304 with sanitary fittings for product-contact surfaces, a 10-inch colour touch, a 5.5–7.5 kW connected load and a 1600 × 1400 × 2400 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Run the integration review with the people who operate the line, not only the people who install it. The small scale juice bottling line is mechanically compatible with common conveyors, but the small beverage startups workflow decides how it is actually used.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The strongest ROI argument for a small scale juice bottling line is downtime avoided, not speed gained. At 5424 units per hour with a 15-minute changeover, the small beverage startups production line recovers exactly what the changeover takes away.

Maintenance and Service

The small scale juice bottling line was designed so the routine work happens during a changeover rather than during production. Seals, sensors and drives are grouped for access, which is what allows a 5424 unit per hour small beverage startups production line to maintain without a shutdown.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are changeovers recorded for traceability?

The 10-inch colour touch stores the recipe per format and the parameters can be exported. For a small beverage startups operation running audits, request the export format in writing.

Does the small scale juice bottling line need a specific foundation?

No special foundation is required beyond a level floor with the clearance shown on the 1600 × 1400 × 2400 mm GA drawing. Confirm the floor loading for heavy configurations.

What is the actual connected load of the small scale juice bottling line?

The reference build draws 5.5–7.5 kW. Confirm the starting current and the supply arrangement at the site before ordering.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
