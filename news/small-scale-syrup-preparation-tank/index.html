Small Scale Syrup Preparation Tank

Published 2026-11-28 · KIWL Machine

A small scale syrup preparation tank for small beverage startups is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

The small scale syrup preparation tank is where raw material becomes a finished unit, and that conversion is where a small beverage startups operation's cost is set. At 2646–3396 units per hour for 330 ml product, this build makes that conversion repeatable.

Key Technical Specifications

The specification that gets missed is the changeover. A small scale syrup preparation tank with a 15-minute changeover across 330 ml format and food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts is a different proposition from one without, even at the same 2646–3396 units per hour.

Integration With Your Line

Plan upstream buffer, downstream transfer and shared CIP or control logic so the small scale syrup preparation tank does not become the bottleneck. On this build the machine accepts a standard encoder interface and a 1600 mm working width, making it straightforward to index against existing small beverage startups conveyors.

Capacity Planning and ROI

The payback case for a small scale syrup preparation tank is arithmetic: every unit of 0.3 ml deviation that disappears is material recovered, and every minute of 15-minute changeover that disappears is production time returned to the small beverage startups production line.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a small scale syrup preparation tank with food-grade 304 stainless steel contact parts and 2646–3396 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.
