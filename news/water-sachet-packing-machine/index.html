Where a water sachet packing machine specification is thin, it is usually thin on the figures that decide the outcome. This guide lays out the small beverage startups requirements in the form of numbers, then explains why each of them matters.

What This Equipment Does

For a small beverage startups operation, the water sachet packing machine is where a production target either becomes repeatable or stays theoretical. This unit runs at 5845–7345 units per hour on 250–500 ml product with a measured deviation rather than a quoted one.

Key Technical Specifications

A water sachet packing machine is properly specified when an auditor can check it. Rated capacity 5845–7345 units per hour, contact parts 304 stainless steel, control 4-inch HMI, load 5.5–7.5 kW, footprint 3200 × 1900 × 2800 mm, changeover 12 minutes — this build carries all six.

Integration With Your Line

Physical integration is the easy half. The harder half is whether the small beverage startups team will adopt the routine — and a water sachet packing machine with a 12-minute changeover is far easier to adopt than one that needs a technician.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A water sachet packing machine at 5845–7345 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 250–500 ml product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the small beverage startups operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

Documentation is part of maintenance. Ask for the exploded view, the seal change procedure and the control parameter backup, because a water sachet packing machine with 304 stainless steel contact parts and 5845–7345 units per hour is not a machine your team can improvise on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the water sachet packing machine documented for the buyer?

The documentation set covers the exploded view, electrical drawings, the parameter backup procedure and the spare parts list with lead times.

What spare parts should be held for the water sachet packing machine?

The wear items are seals, sensors and drives. For a line running 5845 units per hour, holding these on site protects availability.

Can the water sachet packing machine run without a dedicated operator?

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
